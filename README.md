## The Idea

Meesho Next addresses two key frictions limiting Beauty & Personal Care creator-commerce:

### Trial Stock
Brand-funded product trials that reduce creator product-access friction and enable authentic content creation.

### Proof Match
Matches relevant creator proof to buyers based on their need, suitability, language and shopping context.

> **More relevant products → More authentic proof → Better buyer confidence → More transactions**

## Proof Match

Proof Match combines buyer, product and creator signals to surface the most relevant creator proof for each buyer.

Key signals include:

- Buyer need
- Product attributes
- Language
- Suitability
- Creator expertise
- Product experience
- Audience similarity
- Historical performance

### Same product. Different proof.

The same product can surface different creator proof depending on who the buyer is and what they need.

---

## Pilot Experiment

The prototype models a controlled **2×2 factorial experiment**:

| | No Proof Match | Proof Match |
|---|---|---|
| **No Trial Stock** | Control | Proof Match |
| **Trial Stock** | Trial Stock | Combined |

This isolates:

- Trial Stock → creator and content supply
- Proof Match → buyer conversion
- Combined → incremental NMV and unit economics

### Primary KPI

**Incremental NMV / ₹ Trial Stock Cost**

### Key Metrics

**Supply:** Posts / creator · Active creators · Views

**Conversion:** CTR · CVR · NMV / creator

**Creator health:** Retention · Earnings

**Efficiency:** Cost / incremental order · Return rate · Contribution margin
